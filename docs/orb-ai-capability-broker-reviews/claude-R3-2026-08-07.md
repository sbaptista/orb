### Correction to my own R2 assessment

R2-C3 stated that "D2 and the client-storage half of D9" were not decidable.
That conflated two different dependencies, and the revision is more accurate
than my comment was.

The register now distinguishes them correctly:

- **D2 — `Blocked on evidence`.** The transport choice genuinely cannot be made
  until client authentication and credential containment are verified. Correct.
- **D9 — `Pending`**, annotated: *"Client-storage verification is required
  before selecting the implementation mechanism, not before deciding the
  principle."* Correct, and I was wrong to imply otherwise. D9's proposed choice
  — per-client, scoped, revocable grants stored outside repositories, never a
  shared `ORB_API_SECRET` — holds under either transport, so the principle is
  decidable now and only the mechanism waits.
- **D15** carries the same shape for schedule versus policy.

That three-way distinction between *evidence-blocked*, *mechanism-dependent*,
and *schedule-dependent* is a real improvement to the register and should be
preserved in future revisions.

## Part 2 — Remaining concerns

One, and it is procedural rather than architectural.

### `claude-R3-C1` — **Decision-readiness concern** (scope: §12 preamble, §12 Phase A, D2, D14)

**D14 and Phase A form a circular dependency around D2.**

- **D14:** "No build until all applicable decisions are Decided and document
  status is Approved."
- **§12 preamble:** "Implementation is blocked until Stan marks the relevant
  decisions in section 16 as decided and changes this document's status to
  approved."
- **§12 Phase A, second bullet:** "Decide primary transport and authentication
  **after verifying both clients**."
- **D2:** `Blocked on evidence` — awaiting exactly that client verification.

So D2 requires evidence produced by verifying both clients; that verification is
listed inside Phase A; and Phase A cannot begin until the applicable decisions,
which include D2, are Decided. Read literally, the plan cannot start.

The likely intent is that client-credential verification is investigation rather
than implementation, and therefore permitted before approval. But Phase A also
contains unambiguous build work — freezing schemas, adding a fail-closed
authentication gate, writing canary tests — so "Phase A is not really build" is
not available as a reading. And the ambiguity matters precisely because D14 is
the plan's hard gate: a gate that can be argued around is weaker than one that
cannot.

**Recommendation — either of:**

1. Split the client-credential verification into a **Phase 0**, explicitly
   permitted before approval and producing only evidence (no schema freeze, no
   route, no credential issuance); or
2. Amend D14 to read approximately: *no build until all applicable decisions
   are Decided, except rows marked `Blocked on evidence`, which must be Decided
   before the phase that depends on them.*

Option 1 is cleaner, because it keeps D14 absolute and makes the evidence work
visibly not-a-build. Option 2 is a one-line change if Stan prefers to leave the
phase structure alone.

Either way this is a wording change of a few lines. It does not affect the
architecture, the threat model, or any other decision row.
